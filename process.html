Architectural Methodology

The Design Process

A rigorous, structured four-stage framework designed to deliver creative clarity, cost transparency, and seamless execution.

Transparent Roadmap

Structured phases, calm progression.

A successful interior renovation is as much about process governance as it is about aesthetics. We guide clients through four sequential milestones, ensuring every detail is tested, dimensioned, and costed prior to construction.

01
Stage 01

Consultation & Spatial Discovery

We begin with an in-depth on-site appraisal of the property. We examine natural light movement, ceiling heights, historical moldings, structural constraints, and your daily household rituals.

  • Comprehensive site measure & photographic audit
  • Functional brief and storage requirements definition
  • Budget parameters and project timeline alignment
02
Stage 02

Concept & Material Trays

We translate the spatial brief into visual and tactile reality. We present multiple floorplan iterations accompanied by physical sample boxes containing actual stone, timber, lime plaster, and textile swatches.

  • 2D dimensioned spatial layout options
  • Physical tactile material boxes for touch and feel
  • Atmospheric lighting and color palettes
03
Stage 03

Design Development & Tender Package

The technical foundation of your build. Every centimeter of millwork, stone detailing, electrical placement, and plumbing drop is documented in exhaustive technical drawing packages for contractor tender.

  • 1:20 and 1:10 bespoke joinery drawings
  • Reflected ceiling and electrical switching plans
  • Itemized schedule of finishes and sanitaryware
04
Stage 04

Delivery, Styling & Handover

As construction concludes, our studio oversees site snagging, white-glove furniture delivery, fine art mounting, and textile steaming. We leave you with an enduring room and complete material maintenance manuals.

  • On-site joinery and finish quality review
  • White-glove loose furniture placement and styling
  • Stone, timber, and fabric care manual handover
