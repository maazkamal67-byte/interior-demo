Discipline Overview

Space Planning

Rigorous architectural layout optimization, intuitive circulation paths, and solar daylight alignment before construction starts.

Service Scope

Unlocking the geometric potential of your floor plan.

Exceptional interior architecture begins with an intuitive plan. Space planning analyzes how you move through a property, eliminating bottlenecks and creating natural pauses, focal points, and unobstructed axes of daylight.

What is included in this service:

  • As-built dimensional verification and structural partition assessment
  • Scaled 2D master floor plans with precise wall thicknesses and door swings
  • Circulation and clearance corridor diagrams according to human scale
  • Sightline diagrams framing key architectural views and natural vistas
  • Furniture footprint planning to avoid overcrowded seating groups
  • Multiple layout iterations for client evaluation and contractor tender
Methodology

Our space planning process.

Moving methodically from on-site structural surveys to dimensioned master plans.

01

Site Measurement

Precision laser measuring of perimeter walls, load-bearing pillars, window drops, and ceiling heights.

02

Zoning & Flow

Exploring primary vs. secondary movement corridors, acoustic separation, and daylight penetrations.

03

Options Review

Presenting two to three distinct layout options with detailed pros, cons, and structural implications.

04

Master Package

Final dimensioned CAD/PDF plans ready for submission to structural engineers, builders, and planning teams.
