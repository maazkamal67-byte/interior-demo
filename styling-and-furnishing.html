Discipline Overview

Styling & Furnishing

Imbuing spaces with soul, tactile layers, artisanal ceramics, hand-knotted textiles, and collector-grade furnishings.

Service Scope

The sensory completion of an interior.

Architecture establishes proportions, but loose furnishings and soft textures define how a home feels on a quiet evening. We source distinctive pieces from independent European makers, master weavers, and antique dealers to construct a layered interior identity.

What is included in this service:

  • Curated loose furniture specification from independent studios and heritage makers
  • Bespoke hand-knotted rug design, sizing, and wool/silk yarn matching
  • Textile curation: Belgian linen drapery, bouclé upholstery, and shearling accents
  • Decorative lighting selection: hand-turned table lamps and sculptural pendants
  • Art consultation, custom archival framing, and precision gallery hanging
  • White-glove delivery coordination, unpacking, and shelf styling
Process Sequence

Our furnishing & styling journey.

Four distinct phases from initial mood curation to final placement.

01

Palette & Texture

Curating fabric samples, timber finishes, leather swatches, and rug strike-offs tailored to your rooms.

02

Itemized Schedule

Providing full specifications, dimensions, lead times, and trade procurement pricing with complete clarity.

03

Procurement Management

Handling purchase orders, import documentation, warehouse consolidation, and quality inspections.

04

White-Glove Styling

Coordinated installation day: furniture positioning, steam pressing drapery, art hanging, and accessory placement.
