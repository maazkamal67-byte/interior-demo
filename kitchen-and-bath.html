Discipline Overview

Kitchen & Bath

Culinary workshops and tactile wet-room sanctuaries specified with honest stone, fluted timber, and understated craftsmanship.

Service Scope

Tactile sanctuaries and culinary workspaces.

Kitchens and bathrooms are the sensory heart of modern residences. We design these technical spaces with monolithic natural stone countertops, integrated concealed appliances, and whisper-quiet acoustic finishes.

What is included in this service:

  • Ergonomic kitchen zoning: preparation, cooking, washing, and pantry storage
  • Cabinetry elevations, internal timber drawer organizers, and pocket doors
  • Natural stone quarry sourcing, slab templating, and bullnose or eased edge details
  • Plumbing fixture schedules: unlacquered brassware, cast iron or stone baths
  • Concealed extractor ventilation and waterproof architectural lighting
  • Coordination with joiners, stone fabricators, and MEP contractors
Process Sequence

How our kitchen & bath projects flow.

Four distinct stages from appliance specification to final silicone and stone sealant.

01

Culinary & Wet Brief

Reviewing daily cooking rituals, appliance preferences, shower vs. bath requirements, and storage capacities.

02

Slab & Finish Sourcing

Physical stone yard visits to review limestone, marble, and quartzite slabs, alongside bespoke timber samples.

03

Joinery & MEP Pack

Delivering 1:20 and 1:10 millwork drawings, waste fall calculations, and electrical connection schedules.

04

Templating Review

Laser templating inspection before stone cutting, dry fit oversight, and plumbing commissioning review.
