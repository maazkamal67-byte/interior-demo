Practice Disciplines

Design Services

Comprehensive interior architectural solutions tailored to residential, culinary, and boutique commercial environments.

Disciplined Scope

Holistic spatial design, from blueprint to placement.

Whether undertaking a full residence reimagination, a focused kitchen and bath refurbishment, or a boutique commercial atelier, we provide rigorous technical drawing sets, tactile material curation, and on-site oversight.

Discipline 01

Residential Design

Full-scope interior architectural design for private houses, apartments, and heritage residences. We curate custom floorplans, bespoke millwork, atmospheric lighting schemes, and unified finishes that honor the character of the home.

  • Comprehensive interior architectural drawing packages
  • Custom cabinetry, library walls, and wardrobe joinery
  • Curated finishes schedule: flooring, lime plaster, stone
  • Architectural lighting and electrical placement coordination

Discipline 02

Kitchen & Bath

Dedicated design for the home’s most tactile, hard-working environments. We balance high culinary ergonomics with monolithic natural stone islands, custom pantry solutions, and quiet spa wet rooms.

  • Appliance integration and culinary workflow zoning
  • Natural stone slab selection, bookmatching, and edge detailing
  • Sanitaryware, unlacquered brassware, and drainage specifications
  • Moisture-resistant bespoke vanities and integrated mirror cabinets

Discipline 03

Space Planning

Before a single wall is moved or furniture selected, our space planning service evaluates structural flow, sightlines, solar exposure, and circulation corridors to maximize the organic potential of your floor plan.

  • Structural wall partition analysis and doorway alignments
  • 2D dimensioned floor plans and circulation studies
  • Daylight mapping and natural ventilation considerations
  • Multi-functional room zoning for hybrid work and living

Discipline 04

Styling & Furnishing

Infusing spaces with layered soul, tactility, and personality. We procure enduring loose furniture, bespoke wool rugs, vintage lighting pieces, architectural drapery, and contemporary craft objects that complete the architectural shell.

  • Bespoke rug sizing, weaving commissions, and fabric selection
  • Window treatments: linen sheers, blackout blinds, drapery
