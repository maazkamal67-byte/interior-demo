Discipline Overview

Commercial Interiors

Understated executive suites, client reception areas, and creative studios planned for acoustic intimacy and timeless brand presentation.

Service Scope

Workspaces with residential warmth and commercial rigor.

Contemporary workplaces require more than sterile open desks. We design boutique commercial spaces that feel welcoming, acoustically controlled, and deeply considered, creating an enduring environment for team collaboration and confidential client meetings.

What is included in this service:

  • Boutique reception, executive boardroom, and hospitality pantry layouts
  • Acoustic engineering: concealed wall absorbers, fabric panels, and quiet pods
  • Commercial contract-grade finish schedules (high-traffic timber, durable stone)
  • Audiovisual joinery integration, cable pathways, and discreet power modules
  • Emergency egress, fire regulation compliance, and accessibility review
  • Contractor tender documentation and landlord approvals liaison
Process Sequence

Our commercial project structure.

Four focused phases balancing brand atmosphere with building code compliance.

01

Capacity & Brand Brief

Headcount requirements, visitor flows, acoustic privacy needs, and brand identity alignment.

02

Spatial Concept

Reception arrival sequence, presentation suite layouts, and high-performance material boards.

03

Tender Package

Detailed technical drawings, MEP integration plans, door schedules, and building authority documentation.

04

Fit-Out Handover

Contractor coordination, millwork defect inspection, acoustic validation, and key handover.
