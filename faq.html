Project duration is determined by physical scale and construction complexity. Design and documentation generally require 6 to 10 weeks across Concept and Detailed Design phases. Construction, joinery manufacture, and procurement depend upon builder programs, typically spanning 3 to 9 months for residential refurbishments.

Yes. We regularly work alongside client-appointed structural engineers, planning consultants, architects, and main contractors. Our technical drawing sets are formatted for seamless integration into multi-disciplinary construction packages.

We operate on a transparent phased fixed design fee determined during the initial consultation. Each phase (Discovery, Concept, Detailed Documentation, and Site Handover) has defined deliverables and milestone billing, ensuring total budget visibility.

The ideal stage is as early as possible—ideally alongside or shortly after architectural floor plan drafting. Early interior involvement ensures electrical drops, plumbing runs, ceiling recesses, and door alignments reflect your intended furniture layout before concrete is poured or stud walls are erected.

Material honesty and indoor air quality are fundamental to our practice. We routinely specify breathable mineral lime plasters, certified sustainably harvested European hardwoods, natural wool insulation, and zero-VOC water-based finishes.

For styling and furnishing commissions, we prepare an itemized specification schedule detailing trade discounts, freight, and production lead times. We manage purchase orders, warehouse consolidation, and white-glove site delivery on your behalf.
