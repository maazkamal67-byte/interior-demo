Discipline Overview

Residential Design

Harmonious interior architectural environments tailored for private living, from historic renovations to contemporary townhouses.

Service Scope

Complete residential interior architecture.

Our residential design service takes responsibility for the complete interior environment. We address the home as an interconnected whole, ensuring that transitions between living zones, private chambers, and service areas feel natural, calm, and grounded.

What is included in this service:

  • Comprehensive interior architectural layout and partition plans
  • Full millwork drawing sets for bespoke wardrobes, storage, and bookcases
  • Hard finishes schedule: timber flooring, lime renders, architectural stone
  • Reflected ceiling and architectural lighting placement plans
  • Door schedules, ironmongery, and hardware specifications
  • Regular site reviews to ensure builder alignment with drawing sets
The Framework

How residential projects progress.

A four-phase structure ensuring clarity, technical precision, and calm execution.

01

Discovery & Brief

Initial site assessment, measurement verification, architectural review, and discussion of your lifestyle rituals.

02

Spatial Concept

Detailed 2D layout plans, atmospheric mood boards, tactile material trays, and spatial volume studies.

03

Technical Package

Comprehensive joinery elevations, electrical and plumbing schematics, and contractor-ready finish schedules.

04

On-Site Oversight

Regular site inspections, answering contractor technical queries, joinery snagging, and handover review.
